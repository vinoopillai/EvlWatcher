### 2026-11 release of v3.4.0
- Bans & Whitelist tab: a search box above the three lists. Type any part of an address or pattern (e.g. 203.0.113, 192.168 or db8)
  and the temporary bans, permanent bans and whitelist show only matching entries; "4 of 9 entries match." tells you how many.
  The count badges and all buttons still work on the full lists ("Ban all permanently" bans every temporary ban, shown or not).
  The search looks for the typed text: a full IP such as 192.168.1.5 does not find the whitelist pattern 192.168.* that covers it.
- status bar: "Last activity" shows the service's newest log message with its age (e.g. "3 min ago: Temporarily banning 203.0.113.7,
  this is strike 1"), and on the right the age of the IP country data used by rules with allowed countries ("updated today",
  "9 days old", "not loaded"). It turns amber when the data is missing or more than 14 days old: see Settings > Country data.
- console only; the service is unchanged from v3.3.0. Note: a v3.4 console with a service older than v3.3 hides the country data item.
- everything else as in v3.3.0 (country filter), v3.2.0 (Analyzer tab) and v3.1.0 (Windows 8.1 / Server 2012 or later with .NET Framework 4.7.2).
