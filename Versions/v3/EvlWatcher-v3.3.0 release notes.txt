### 2026-11 release of v3.3.0
- new option on each rule (Rules tab): "Only allow addresses from: IN" (one or more two-letter country codes, e.g. IN or IN, AE).
  A failed attempt from any other country gets the rule's temporary ban on its FIRST attempt instead of after "5 within 60 minutes".
  Addresses from the allowed countries keep the rule's normal limit, so attackers from those countries are still caught the usual way.
- country bans are temporary only and never count towards a permanent ban. A foreign address is banned again on its next attempt, and
  while its old attempt is still inside the rule's time window it is banned again as soon as the ban lifts.
- the country is the one an address is REGISTERED in, not where the person is. Users on cloud servers (e.g. a Mumbai region registered
  abroad), VPNs or roaming abroad can get a temporary ban after one mistyped password: put their addresses on the whitelist.
  The whitelist, private/LAN addresses and this server's own addresses are never country-banned.
- country data: the free statistics files of the five internet registries (APNIC, RIPE NCC, ARIN, LACNIC, AFRINIC), about 330,000 ranges.
  The installer ships a copy in the geo folder; the service refreshes it weekly by itself. This needs outgoing HTTPS from the server to
  ftp.apnic.net, ftp.ripe.net, ftp.arin.net, ftp.lacnic.net and ftp.afrinic.net (normally allowed). A registry that can't be reached
  keeps its previous copy. If no country data can be loaded at all, every rule simply uses its normal limit.
  Settings tab: "Country data" shows the date and any problem, with "Update weekly" and "Update now".
- the option is off for every rule until you switch it on. Upgrades keep your config.xml.
- everything else as in v3.2.0 (Analyzer tab) and v3.1.0 (Windows 8.1 / Server 2012 or later with .NET Framework 4.7.2).
